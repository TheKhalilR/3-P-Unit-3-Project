Grilled Cheese

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1

Ingredients Quantity Price
Cheese one pack $5.00
Bread one loaf $3.00
Butter One tub $4.00

Step 1:

  • Go to the store and buy all the items you need.

    • Step 2:

  • Go to the kitchen and prepare the ingredients.

    • Step 3:

  • Butter the pan thoroughly and put the bread and cheese on it.

    • Step 4:

  • Cook the grilled cheese on both sides until golden brown.

    • Step 5:

  • Serve the grilled cheese hot and enjoy!
