Grilled Cheese

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1

Ingredients Quantity Price
Cheese one pack $5.00
Bread one loaf $3.00
Butter One tub $4.00

Step 1:

  • Go to the store and buy all the items you need.

    • Step 2:

  • Go to the kitchen and prepare the ingredients.

    • Step 3:

  • Butter the pan thoroughly and put the bread and cheese on it.

    • Step 4:

  • Cook the grilled cheese on both sides until golden brown.

    • Step 5:

  • Serve the grilled cheese hot and enjoy!

    • Alfredo

    • Time: 30-60 minutes
    • Serves: 4
    Ingredients Quantity Price
    Fettuccine noodles Two boxes $3.50
    Alfredo sauce One or two jars $3.00-7.00
    Chicken One pack $6.00-10.00
    Parmesan Cheese One Tub $4.00
    Seasonings As needed $10.00

    Step 1:

  • Bring a pot of water to boil then put in the noodles until soft

    • Step 2:

  • Clean chicken and soak in lemon juice, after season the chicken and put it on a pan to cook

    • Step 3:

  • After prepping your ingrediants, put the noodles in a pot and mix in alfredo sauce and cheese

    • Step 4:

  • Make sure the cheese melts then serve with the chicken and enjoy!

    • Street Tacos

    • Time: 30 minutes
    • Serves: 4
    Ingredients Quantity Price
    Tortillas One pack $3.00
    Ground beef One pound $5.00
    Lettuce One medium $3.00
    Tomatoes Two medium $2.00
    Cheese One pack $5.00
    Sour cream One container $3.00
    Guacamole One tub $7.00

    Step 1:

  • Put a pan on the stove with oil coating the bottom

    • Step 2:

    While the oil heats up cut up your ingrediants and cook the ground beef

    Step 3:

  • When the oil is hot enough put a tortilla and cheese with a top on it

    • Step 4:

  • When the cheese melts add the rest of your ingrediants and fold the tortilla over

    • Step 5:

  • When both sides are cooked and golden brown serve and enjoy!
